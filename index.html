Official evidence: URSC: Dr M Annadurai ↗

Born in Kodhawady, educated in Coimbatore, and shaped by 36 years at ISRO. A career dedicated to helping turn a nation's ambition into journeys beyond Earth.

Project director of Chandrayaan-1. Former director of the ISRO Satellite Centre. Later work includes advising innovators and speaking on science and education.
