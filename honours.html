Lifetime Achievement Award for space science

Recognition

Tamil Nadu honours its space scientists

Dr. Annadurai was among nine space scientists felicitated by Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M. K. Stalin for their service to the nation. At the ceremony, the Chief Minister announced a cash award of ₹25 lakh for each scientist.

Published 2 October 2023

Recognition

Coimbatore celebrates its Padma Shri recipients

The Indian Chamber of Commerce and Industry felicitated Dr. Annadurai and social entrepreneur Arunachalam Muruganantham in Coimbatore after they received the Padma Shri. Annadurai reflected on the encouragement he had received as a child.
