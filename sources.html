Reading the record carefully

Official references were checked on 3 October 2026 and linked beside chronology entries, selected honours and page topics. A mission page supports mission facts, not an individual’s tenure. Every career and honours entry now states its verification status; collection-only dates are not presented as independently confirmed. The official URSC biography independently supports several education, career and award claims.

  • H.K. Firodia Award: the official URSC biography gives 2007; the supplied honours collection gives 2009. The award provider’s year-by-year register confirms 2009, which this edition adopts.
  • Education: the profile uses “Government College of Engineering”; the chronology names Government College of Technology, Coimbatore. This edition follows the chronology. The PhD year is not recorded; honorary doctorates are listed separately.
  • INSAT-3B: the English chronology names INSAT-3B (2000–2010); the Tamil record uses the designation INSAT-3P. Both editions now use INSAT-3B; the personal tenure still relies on the collection.
  • Edutec4 Space: the English title is Patron; the Tamil text describes a senior advisor.
  • Dhaksha Unmanned Systems: English dates are 2023–2026; the Tamil record gives 2024 onwards. Neither discrepancy has been independently reconciled.
  • Honours: BHASKARA (2016) and SIES National Eminence (2009) are separate honours. The 2024 Weekend Leader / Ethiraj College award appears in two categories: 79 entries represent 78 distinct recognitions.
  • Unknown dates: no year is inferred from “AISYWC-18”, “76th Indian Science Congress” or an anniversary. The unconfirmed UCLA range is retained internally but excluded from the displayed year filters.
  • Different award records: the Tamil collection has fewer entries. It dates the Outstanding Personality Award to 24 May 2024; the English inventory gives 25 May. Both editions show May 2024 and flag the conflicting days.
  • Books, photographs and family: 40 image filenames survive in the inventory, but the photographs are unavailable here. Filenames do not establish book titles, identities or dates. A separate researched bookshelf now supplies catalogue-backed titles and cover thumbnails. The family collection records a “Speech/address by Ayya’s father”; no recording URL is available.

  • IIITDM develops integrated machine that can process banana stem to make fibre yarn · EdexLive / IANS. Annadurai spearheaded an industry–institute initiative in which IIITDM faculty and students developed a banana pseudo-stem processing machine; Gencrest planned an associated pilot plant. Contemporary reporting of development and plans, not evidence of later commercial performance.
  • Chairmen of NDRF · National Design and Research Forum. The past-chairmen register lists Dr. M. Annadurai from 18 February 2019 to 7 March 2022. The institutional register’s dates and organization name are used in both editions; the original collection remains preserved.
  • Chandrayaan-1 / Moon Impact Probe · NASA Science. The mission combined Indian instruments with scientific equipment from the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Sweden and Bulgaria; NASA’s M3 helped confirm water locked in lunar minerals. Mission context and shared scientific contribution, not a claim of sole discovery by Annadurai.

NDRF discrepancy: the institutional register gives 18 February 2019–7 March 2022, while the biographical collection gives 2019–2023 and reverses “Design” and “Research” in the organization name. Both editions now use the institutional register’s dates and corrected organization name.

Excluded claims: one podcast description incorrectly calls Annadurai a former ISRO chairman. This biography uses his satellite-centre directorship. Patent US7185858B2 lists other inventors and is not attributed to him. A republication of the banana-fibre report is not treated as a second independent confirmation.
