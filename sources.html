Further reading, talks and interviews

Eight additional recordings were selected from original publisher uploads on 2 October 2026, supplementing two existing links. This English edition presents eight recordings; the Tamil edition presents the two Tamil interviews. Selection considered relevance, publisher provenance and range of perspectives. View counts are dated snapshots, not audience ratings.

Recording cards show publisher titles, upload dates and durations. Publication timestamps use Indian Standard Time. Source links appear on the recording cards.

  • IIITDM develops integrated machine that can process banana stem to make fibre yarn · EdexLive / IANS. Annadurai spearheaded an industry–institute initiative in which IIITDM faculty and students developed a banana pseudo-stem processing machine; Gencrest planned an associated pilot plant. Contemporary reporting of development and plans, not evidence of later commercial performance.
  • Chairmen of NDRF · National Design and Research Forum. The past-chairmen register lists Dr. M. Annadurai from 18 February 2019 to 7 March 2022. The institutional register’s dates and organization name are used in both editions; the original collection remains preserved.
  • Chandrayaan-1 / Moon Impact Probe · NASA Science. The mission combined Indian instruments with scientific equipment from the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Sweden and Bulgaria; NASA’s M3 helped confirm water locked in lunar minerals. Mission context and shared scientific contribution, not a claim of sole discovery by Annadurai.

NDRF discrepancy: the institutional register gives 18 February 2019–7 March 2022, while the biographical collection gives 2019–2023 and reverses “Design” and “Research” in the organization name. Both editions now use the institutional register’s dates and corrected organization name.

Events and recognition in the press

These original summaries follow reporting by The Hindu, The Times of India and The Economic Times / PTI, reviewed on 3 October 2026. Dates on the cards identify article publication, not necessarily the event date. The selection is not a complete event history.

  • Inspiring young space explorers · The Times of India · 28 September 2026. Reports participation in a completed regional congress, not the later state-level conference.
  • Lifetime Achievement Award for space science · The Hindu · 10 March 2024. Confirms the award already listed in the collection; publication date is not asserted as the ceremony date.
  • Bringing space technology into healthcare · The Times of India · 20 January 2024. Reports his address and proposed applications, not clinical outcomes.
  • Tamil Nadu honours its space scientists · The Economic Times / PTI · 2 October 2023. Confirms felicitation and an announced cash award; does not establish payment.
  • Supporting hands-on science in schools · The Times of India · 9 August 2023. Confirms presence at the opening; does not attribute inauguration to Annadurai or claim projected training targets were achieved.
