01 / ROOTS

Early life
& education.

Aerospace engineer, former ISRO scientist and recipient of the Padma Shri (2016).

Born on 2 July 1958 in Kodhawady, Tamil Nadu. Schooling in the village, completed in 1976, laid the foundation for an engineering education: Electronics and Communication Engineering at the Government College of Technology, Coimbatore (1976–1980), followed by a master's degree in Applied Electronics at PSG College of Technology (1980–1982) and a PhD from Anna University. Honorary doctorates are listed separately in the honours archive.

A career at the Indian Space Research Organisation began in 1982. Work on satellite simulation and spacecraft operations became the foundation for a career in mission leadership.

02 / LEADERSHIP

Building missions.
Building teams.

Assignments spanned the IRS and INSAT satellite programmes, EDUSAT and lunar exploration. Project leadership of Chandrayaan-1 (2004–2009) brought together the team behind India's first lunar mission.

Responsibilities included project direction of Chandrayaan-2 during its development (2008–2013), programme direction for Indian Remote Sensing and Small, Science and Student Satellites (2011–2015), and directorship of the ISRO Satellite Centre, now the U R Rao Satellite Centre (1 April 2015–31 July 2018).

04 / STILL LOOKING FORWARD

Science in
everyday life.

After retirement from ISRO, advisory work continued with startups, charities and institutions. Institutional profiles record service as vice president of the Tamil Nadu State Council for Science and Technology and advisory work in Earth observation, biotechnology, aerospace and education. The council role’s end date differs between sources; the chronology explains the conflict.

Service as chairman of the Board of Governors of the National Design and Research Forum spanned 18 February 2019 to 7 March 2022. Further institutional profiles record board membership at SS Innovations and advisory connections with the American India Foundation.

A Kaynes Technology stock-exchange disclosure dates the appointment as Additional Non-Executive Independent Director to 13 May 2026. Reporting on 29 June 2026 also confirms the appointment to chair Tamil Nadu’s curriculum design committee.

Engineering beyond space

In January 2021, EdexLive reported on leadership of a banana-fibre initiative. Faculty and students at IIITDM Kancheepuram developed an integrated machine to process banana pseudo-stems, working with industry partner Gencrest. The report described prototype development and plans for a pilot plant; it did not establish later commercial results.
